A small corner of the internet I own end-to-end.

## Stack

- **Astro** for content-first pages and view transitions.
- **Tailwind v4** with a tiny token set in `global.css`.
- **Biome** for formatting, **Lucide** for icons.

## Why bother

I wanted a place to think out loud without a CMS, a comment system, or a
build step that takes longer than the thought itself. Markdown files in,
HTML out, no analytics in the way.
