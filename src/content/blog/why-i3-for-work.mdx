I've been using i3 as my primary window manager for work, and the switch has been more impactful than I expected.

## What is i3?

i3 is a tiling window manager for Linux. Instead of floating windows you drag around, windows snap into a grid that you control with the keyboard.

## Why it works for me

- **Keyboard-driven.** My hands stay on the home row. No more reaching for the mouse to snap a window to the left half of the screen.
- **Minimal chrome.** No title bars, no docks, no trays — just the content.
- **Fast.** It's small, written in C, and starts in milliseconds.
